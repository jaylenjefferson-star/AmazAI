# Billing, Cancellation & Refund Policy

**In short:** Paid subscriptions renew until canceled. We show the price, billing period, included credits, and material limits before checkout. You can manage or cancel a subscription through the Stripe-hosted billing portal. Except where law requires otherwise, charges and credit purchases are non-refundable.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to paid AmazAI subscriptions, credit purchases, trials, and other fees charged by AmazFlow, LLC, doing business as AmazAI ("AmazAI," "we," "us," or "our"). It supplements our [Terms of Use](/terms). A signed order form or enterprise agreement controls if it states different billing terms.

## 2. Plans, prices, and credits

Current plan prices, billing periods, included Amaz Credits, and material plan limits are shown on the [Pricing page](/pricing) and again at checkout. Checkout terms control if a displayed summary conflicts with the final order presented for purchase. Taxes may be added where required.

Amaz Credits represent prepaid capacity for metered agent work. Model calls, tool use, computer use, and scheduled work may consume credits at different rates. Approval decisions and read-only account views do not themselves consume credits. The Billing screen shows the current balance and recent credit activity.

Unless checkout or a signed order states otherwise, unused purchased credits remain in the account balance while the account is active. Promotional or trial credits may expire or be limited as disclosed when granted. Credits have no cash value, are not legal tender, and cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash except where required by law.

## 3. Free access and trials

We may offer free access or a promotional credit grant without requiring a payment card. A free trial ends when its included capacity is used or when the disclosed trial period ends. We will not convert a no-card trial into a paid subscription unless you affirmatively select a paid plan and authorize payment.

## 4. Recurring subscriptions

Paid subscriptions are billed in advance for the period shown at checkout and automatically renew for successive periods until canceled. By subscribing, you authorize AmazAI and its payment processor to charge the payment method associated with your billing account for recurring fees, applicable taxes, and purchases you approve.

If a payment fails, we or our payment processor may retry the charge, ask you to update the payment method, limit paid features, or suspend new agent work until the account is current. We do not charge an undisclosed fee merely because a payment fails.

## 5. Plan and price changes

An upgrade may take effect immediately and may result in a prorated charge or credit displayed by the billing portal. A downgrade ordinarily takes effect on the date shown in the portal. We will provide advance notice of a material recurring price increase when required by law or contract. Continuing the subscription after the new price takes effect authorizes the renewal at that price, subject to your right to cancel before renewal.

## 6. Cancellation

You may cancel a subscription from **Billing → Manage subscription**, which opens the Stripe-hosted billing portal, or by contacting **billing@amazai.co** if you cannot access the portal. The portal displays when cancellation will take effect. Unless it states otherwise or applicable law requires a different result, cancellation stops the next renewal and paid access continues through the current prepaid period.

Canceling a subscription does not automatically close the AmazAI account or delete Workspace Content. Account closure and deletion are separate actions governed by the [Privacy Policy](/privacy). If credits are exhausted or paid features end, agents may stop new work even while account data remains available.

## 7. Refunds

Except where required by law or a signed order, subscription fees, credit purchases, and other charges are non-refundable and we do not provide prorated refunds for unused time or unused credits.

If AmazAI terminates a prepaid subscription without cause or permanently discontinues the purchased Service before the end of the prepaid period, we will provide a prorated refund for the unused portion. If you believe a charge was made in error, contact **billing@amazai.co** promptly with the account email, charge date, and amount. This section does not limit non-waivable rights under applicable law.

## 8. Payment processing

Stripe processes checkout, saved payment methods, invoices, and the customer billing portal. AmazAI receives payment and subscription records needed to administer the account, but does not receive or store the full payment-card number. Stripe handles information under its own terms and privacy policy and as described in our [Subprocessor List](/subprocessors).

## 9. Contact

Billing and refund questions may be sent to **billing@amazai.co**. Privacy questions should be sent to **privacy@amazai.co**.
