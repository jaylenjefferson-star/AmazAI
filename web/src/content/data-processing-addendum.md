# Data Processing Addendum

**In short:** This page describes the data-processing terms AmazAI offers to business customers. It becomes binding only when an order form or other written agreement says that this Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is incorporated into that agreement.

## 1. Scope and roles

This DPA applies when AmazFlow, LLC, doing business as AmazAI ("AmazAI"), processes personal information in Workspace Content on behalf of a customer under a written agreement that incorporates this DPA.

For that processing, the customer acts as controller or business and AmazAI acts as processor or service provider, as those terms are defined by applicable data-protection law. AmazAI may act as an independent controller for account administration, billing, security, abuse prevention, legal compliance, and its direct business relationship with the customer as described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 2. Customer instructions

AmazAI will process covered personal information only on the customer’s documented instructions, including instructions expressed through authorized use of the Service, and as necessary to provide, secure, support, and maintain the Service or comply with law. The customer is responsible for the lawfulness of its instructions, notices, permissions, and use of connected accounts.

## 3. Processing details

- **Subject matter:** providing AI companions, rooms, routines, files, approvals, connectors, and related support.
- **Duration:** the term of the applicable agreement plus the deletion and backup periods described in the Privacy Policy or order form.
- **Nature and purpose:** hosting, organizing, retrieving, generating, transmitting, and securing content and executing customer-authorized tool actions.
- **Individuals:** customer personnel, users, customers, prospects, vendors, and other people whose information the customer places in or directs the Service to access.
- **Data:** identity and contact information, business records, communications, files, connector content, instructions, outputs, approval records, and other personal information selected by the customer.

The Service is not approved for protected health information, payment-card data, government identification numbers, or other specially regulated data unless a signed order expressly approves that data and configuration.

## 4. Confidentiality and security

AmazAI will ensure that personnel authorized to process covered personal information are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations. AmazAI will maintain administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect covered personal information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Current public information is available in [Security & Responsible Disclosure](/security-disclosure). Customer-specific security commitments must be stated in the applicable order or security exhibit.

## 5. Subprocessors

The customer authorizes AmazAI to use the providers listed on the [Subprocessor List](/subprocessors). AmazAI will impose data-protection obligations on subprocessors appropriate to the services they perform. A customer entitled to advance notice or an objection process will receive it as stated in the applicable agreement.

## 6. Assistance and requests

Taking into account the nature of processing and information available to AmazAI, AmazAI will reasonably assist the customer with requests from individuals, security obligations, impact assessments, regulator inquiries, and breach notifications when required by applicable law and the agreement. Unless law requires AmazAI to respond directly, the customer is responsible for responding to requests concerning customer-controlled Workspace Content.

## 7. Security incidents

After confirming unauthorized access to or disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction of covered personal information, AmazAI will notify the affected customer without undue delay and as required by applicable law or contract. As information becomes reasonably available, notice will describe the known nature and scope, affected data, likely consequences, containment or remediation steps, and a contact for follow-up. Notice is not an admission of fault or liability.

## 8. Return and deletion

During the agreement, the customer may use available Service features to access or export Workspace Content. Following account closure or a valid deletion instruction, AmazAI will delete covered personal information from active systems within the period stated in the Privacy Policy or order form, subject to legal holds, security needs, dispute preservation, and isolated backup cycles. AmazAI will require applicable subprocessors to delete covered data as required by its agreements and applicable law.

## 9. International processing

AmazAI is based in the United States. If applicable law requires a transfer mechanism for processing in another country, the parties will use a legally recognized mechanism identified in the order form or a separate written addendum. This page does not claim participation in a transfer framework or a specific data-residency commitment.

## 10. Audit information

AmazAI will make reasonably available information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, subject to confidentiality, security, and reasonable scope limitations. Any audit right, frequency, cost allocation, report access, or on-site review must be stated in the applicable agreement. AmazAI does not claim a certification or attestation that is not expressly identified in writing.

## 11. Order of precedence and contact

If this DPA conflicts with the applicable agreement regarding covered processing, this DPA controls unless the signed order expressly states otherwise. Other conflicts are governed by the applicable agreement.

To request incorporation of this DPA or discuss data-processing terms, contact **privacy@amazai.co**.
